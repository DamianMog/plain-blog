Hi, I'm Alex. I'm a freelance software engineer who likes small tools, readable code and projects that are still maintainable five years later.

This is a **demo site**. Alex Morgan does not exist, and every post, job and link on these pages is placeholder text that shows what the `plain-blog` template can do. Replace it with your own story by editing the Markdown files in `src/content/pages`.

## What you'll find here

- **Blog posts** about software, tooling and the occasional off-topic obsession.
- **Expertise** and **Experience** pages, which are plain Markdown too, so there is no layout to fight with.
- **No tracking.** No analytics, no cookies, no third-party requests. Even the font is served from this site.

## Outside of work

Long walks, paper notebooks, and trying to bake a decent loaf of bread every weekend. The bread is improving. The handwriting is not.

> I would rather publish ten small, honest pages than one polished page that never ships.

If something here is useful to you, the [blog](/blog/) is the best place to start, and the [contact page](/contact/) is the best place to say hello.
